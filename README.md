# 🍺 The Tavern Lore Keeper

*Every tavern has a story — the keeper has written most of them down.*

A rumor-mill and flavor generator for your D&D table: taverns, rumors, mysterious patrons, and cursed items, all in literary voice.

**Live site:** https://tavern-lore-keeper.pages.dev

---

## How it works

Four generators, one page:

- **A tavern** — *"The Weeping Serpent — where the coins on the counter are always slightly warm."*
- **A rumor** — *"They say the man who drinks alone has been dead for years and is still paying rent…"*
- **A patron** — a look, plus a secret (*"they are looking for someone who is not here yet — and they think it is you"*).
- **A cursed item** — *"The Glass of Hindsight shows you the face of the last person who betrayed you — it is usually your own."*

Or hit **the evening's offering** — one of each, instantly, for campaign flavor on demand.
